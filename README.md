### 🌊 OceanEmbed — Ocean Intelligence

**Satellite Embedding-Based Deep Learning Framework**

A deep learning prototype for reconstructing subsurface ocean temperature profiles from satellite-derived surface observations.

- Dual-encoder architecture combining CNN and Vision Transformer features
- Temperature profile estimation at 15 depth levels
- Thermocline estimation and underwater acoustic analysis
- Interactive ocean map and visualization dashboard
- **Stack:** Python, PyTorch, FastAPI, React, xarray

[Explore Repository →](https://github.com/ag0611/oceanembed)

### 🧠 AI COO — Autonomous Business Operations

An AI-powered business operating system that orchestrates specialized agents to transform business objectives into actionable strategies.

- Agent orchestration for research and business planning
- Policy Guard for validating payment requests
- x402-powered payment workflow
- Executive-ready business reports
- **Stack:** JavaScript, React, Node.js, Express, Groq, MongoDB

[Explore Repository →](https://github.com/ag0611/ai-coo) · [Live Demo](https://ai-coo-blond.vercel.app)

### 🦠 Vector Disease AI — Disease Risk Intelligence

An AI-powered vector-borne disease surveillance platform designed to analyze epidemiological and environmental data for disease-risk intelligence.

- Risk prediction and hotspot analysis
- Historical disease and weather feature engineering
- Machine learning models and risk-driver insights
- Interactive dashboard and AI assistant
- **Stack:** Python, scikit-learn, FastAPI, Next.js, TypeScript

[Explore Repository →](https://github.com/ag0611/vector-disease-ai)

### 🌐 Chain Guard — Supply Chain Risk Intelligence

An AI-powered supply-chain risk platform designed to analyze disruption signals and help users explore potential mitigation strategies.

- Global risk visualization
- Multi-signal risk scoring
- AI-generated risk briefs and recommendations
- Scenario simulation and alternative supplier intelligence
- **Stack:** Python, JavaScript, React, Node.js, MongoDB, Groq

[Explore Repository →](https://github.com/ag0611/chain-guard) · [Live Demo](https://chain-guard-ten.vercel.app)
